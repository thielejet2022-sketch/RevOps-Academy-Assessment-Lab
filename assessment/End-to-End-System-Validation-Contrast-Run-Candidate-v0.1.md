# End-to-End Assessment System Validation — Contrast Run

**Status:** Candidate v0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Canonical:** No  
**Instrument:** Funnel Deterioration End-to-End Assessment Instrument Candidate v0.1  
**Dataset:** FD-001-v0.2  
**Methodology:** Assessment Methodology Candidate v0.1  
**Target Role Profile:** VP of Revenue Operations  
**Participant:** Controlled synthetic participant B  
**Purpose:** Test whether the system distinguishes strong L3 diagnostic/execution capability from L4 strategic system-shaping.

## Experimental Boundary
This is a controlled synthetic system test, not a real participant assessment. Participant B is deliberately designed as a strong, practical RevOps Director: analytically disciplined, appropriately cautious, cross-functional in diagnosis, and capable of designing a useful corrective plan, but not naturally inclined to reshape material investment governance or the operating model.

The goal is not to make Participant B weak. The goal is to test whether **good Director-level work remains L3 rather than being inflated to L4 because it is polished and competent.**

# 3. Executive Readout

**Diagnosis:** The revenue engine has a concentrated SMB funnel problem. Paid-social volume expanded while conversion quality deteriorated, and SDR response/handoff performance also weakened. Those issues are associated with lower SMB opportunity conversion and a longer early-stage sales cycle. New ARR is growing only 3% against a 15% plan.

**Evidence vs Hypothesis:** We know paid-social MQL-to-SQL fell sharply, SMB SQL-to-opportunity fell from 29% to 18%, SDR response and SLA performance deteriorated, and low-intent disqualifications increased. We suspect those factors are contributing to the longer SMB cycle, but we have not established exact causality.

**Action:** Run a 30-day SMB correction plan: remove clearly weak paid-social campaigns, correct SDR SLA execution, support the ramping AE cohort, and inspect the funnel weekly using conversion, velocity, cost-per-opportunity, and revenue-versus-plan metrics.

**Restraint:** Do not eliminate paid social, cut the AE cohort, change pricing, or restructure the GTM organization until the targeted correction plan produces more evidence.

## Participant A
- strong diagnosis;

- several L4 judgments;
- observed VP Role Fit: Meets in tested competencies.

- weekly operating dashboard;
- avoided unsupported large changes;
- did not redesign investment governance or materially shape the operating model;
- predominantly L3 judgments;
- observed VP Role Fit: Approaching in tested competencies.

## Discrimination finding
The methodology can **provisionally distinguish**:

> **L3: diagnose and design an effective cross-functional response**

from

> **L4: additionally shape material operating decisions, tradeoffs, standards, governance, architecture, or senior-stakeholder action.**

The distinction did not depend on vocabulary, confidence, length, or discovery of every hidden evidence packet.

# 8. System-Test Audit

## L3/L4 discrimination
**PASS provisionally.** Participant B remained L3 despite polished, competent, executive-ready work.

## Executive-language inflation
**PASS.** Strong communication did not automatically elevate the substantive competencies to L4.

## Insufficient Evidence
**PASS.** Forecasting & Revenue Planning was left unscored despite plan evidence because the observation was too narrow.

## Dynamic-path fairness
**PASS provisionally.** Participant B used a different evidence sequence and did not request CAC, governance, planning assumptions, AE revenue productivity, ACV, or enablement evidence. The participant was not penalized for missing hidden packets.

## Decision Gate
**PASS.** The participant made a bounded decision without perfect information. The gate did not require L4 behavior; it exposed the altitude of the decision actually made.

## Role Fit / Profile Coverage
**DEFECT REPRODUCED AND CLARIFIED.** The instrument can support an **Observed Role Fit** comparison for tested competencies while remaining unable to support **Overall Role Fit** across the full VP profile.

This is now supported by two controlled synthetic runs with different proficiency patterns.

# 9. Validation Verdict

## Five-level proficiency architecture
**SUPPORTED for continued Candidate development.** The L3/L4 seam survived a deliberately strong L3 contrast case.

## Assessment Methodology
**SUPPORTED with a repeatable defect requiring revision:** Role Fit needs explicit treatment of profile coverage.

## Instrument
**SUPPORTED for continued testing.** It discriminated a strategic L4-oriented response from a strong L3 corrective response without requiring exhaustive evidence discovery.

## Dataset
**SUPPORTED for both tested paths.** Different inquiry paths produced sufficient responsive evidence.

## Validity
**NOT ESTABLISHED.** These are synthetic tests designed by the same system. They do not establish independent evaluator reliability, human response validity, predictive validity, hiring validity, certification validity, or demographic fairness.

# 10. Recommended Methodology Change

Evidence from Runs A and B now supports drafting Assessment Methodology Candidate v0.2 with an explicit separation:

### Observed Role Fit
Comparison between demonstrated evidence and the target Role Profile **within the competencies sufficiently observed by the available assessment evidence**.

### Overall Role Fit
Comparison against the target Role Profile only when profile coverage is sufficient for the breadth of the claim. Otherwise record **Insufficient evidence**.

### Profile Coverage
A descriptive statement of which target competencies have sufficient evidence and which remain Insufficient evidence. Do not convert coverage into a numeric percentage yet.

This preserves the existing four-judgment architecture while preventing partial evidence from masquerading as a complete role assessment.

Do not add a fifth participant-performance dimension. Profile Coverage describes the **assessment evidence**, not the participant.
