# Full-Profile Evaluator Calibration — PP-001 / PP-002 / PP-003

**Status:** Candidate v0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Canonical:** No  
**Frozen target:** VP Revenue Operations Role Profile Candidate v0.2  
**Participants:** PP-001, PP-002, PP-003  
**Purpose:** Test whether multiple pseudo-independent evaluator lenses can reach comparable full-profile judgments from the same accumulated evidence, and identify where evaluator discretion remains too high.

## Critical Limitation
This is **pseudo-independent calibration**, not empirical inter-rater reliability.

All evaluator passes are generated inside the same development environment and may share latent assumptions. Agreement here is useful for framework debugging but cannot be reported as validated evaluator reliability.

A real calibration study requires genuinely independent human evaluators who score blinded evidence before reconciliation.

---

# 1. Calibration Design

The three existing participant evidence bundles are treated as fixed evidence records.

Evaluators are not permitted to:
- change the VP target;
- invent role-context modifiers;
- average competency scores;
- infer capability from title or company scale;
- use the original participant Role Fit label as scoring evidence;
- resolve missing evidence in the participant's favor;
- globally downgrade genuine strengths because another competency is weak.

Each evaluator must judge:
1. competency proficiency where material;
2. Confidence;
3. Profile Coverage;
4. Strategic Anchor pattern;
5. Overall Role Fit;
6. decisive evidence;
7. meaningful disagreement or ambiguity.

## Evaluator lenses

### Evaluator A — Evidence Conservative
Requires repeated observable behavior for L4 and gives substantial weight to contradictory or unsustained evidence. Avoids promotion from one strong event.

### Evaluator B — Role-Pattern Integrator
Focuses on whether the total demonstrated capability forms a coherent pattern for the frozen VP role. Allows one competency to contain mixed evidence when the broader pattern supports a level.

### Evaluator C — Adversarial / Anti-Halo
Actively searches for title, polish, specialist, outcome, and scale halo. Tests whether weaknesses are truly role-breaking and whether strong participants are being promoted because they “feel senior.”

These lenses are intentionally different but must use the same framework.

# 6. Competency-Level Disagreement

Role Fit agreement is stronger than exact competency-level agreement.

The most important disagreement occurs for PP-001:
- Operating Cadence: L3 Advanced vs L4;
- Cross-Functional Leadership: L3/L4 boundary vs L4.

This reveals an important property:

> **Evaluator calibration should not require identical competency labels when the underlying evidence is genuinely boundary-level and the final role interpretation remains coherent.**

The goal is disciplined comparable judgment, not artificial unanimity.

# 7. Calibration Findings

## Finding 1 — Below / Approaching / Meets separation survives evaluator-lens variation
The three role labels remain stable even when evaluator philosophy changes.

This is encouraging Candidate evidence that the role architecture is not wholly dependent on one narrow scoring style.

## Finding 2 — Operating Cadence remains the most fragile L3/L4 seam
The recurring question is:

**How much durable institutionalization evidence is required before a mechanism becomes L4 rather than excellent L3 execution?**

The current framework points in the right direction but still permits reasonable disagreement.

## Finding 3 — Role Fit can be more stable than exact competency scores
This is acceptable if:
- disagreements are evidence-grounded;
- Confidence reflects ambiguity;
- no mechanical gate forces one competency disagreement to flip the whole role result;
- evaluators can identify decisive evidence.

## Finding 4 — The anti-halo architecture survives PP-003
Title, scale, L5 specialist capability, polished communication, and successful operational outcomes did not overwhelm a demonstrated role-breaking weakness.

## Finding 5 — The system should not chase perfect agreement
If future evaluators differ by one level on a genuine boundary case but agree on the evidence, uncertainty, and role implication, forcing identical scores may reduce validity rather than improve it.

Calibration should focus first on **why** the judgment differs.

# 8. Recommended Calibration Standard for Human Pilot

Before real participant use, create a short evaluator protocol that requires each evaluator to record:
1. observable evidence cited;
2. competency interpretation;
3. proficiency judgment;
4. Confidence;
5. contradictions/counterevidence;
6. Profile Coverage;
7. Role Fit;
8. decisive evidence for the Role Fit conclusion.

Evaluators should score independently before discussing results.

Reconciliation should distinguish:
- evidence disagreement;
- competency-definition disagreement;
- proficiency-boundary disagreement;
- Confidence disagreement;
- Role Profile disagreement;
- evaluator bias/halo.

Do not resolve disagreement by averaging levels.

# 9. Architecture Decision After Calibration

## Recommendation
**Do not add more core architecture now.**

The Candidate system has achieved:
- competency architecture;
- five-level proficiency spine;
- evidence anchors across multiple competency types;
- multimodal assessment methodology;
- five-experience battery;
- frozen VP Role Profile;
- persistent-participant full-battery synthesis;
- synthetic Below → Approaching → Meets discrimination;
- pseudo-independent evaluator-lens agreement on Overall Role Fit.

The highest-value next step is no longer another framework artifact.

It is to prepare for a **small human pilot / evaluator protocol**, while explicitly keeping the system developmental and non-certifying.

# 10. What Is Still Not Validated

This calibration does not establish:
- independent human inter-rater reliability;
- predictive job-performance validity;
- hiring validity;
- adverse-impact/fairness validation;
- certification validity;
- legal defensibility for employment selection;
- universal VP RevOps role requirements;
- numeric score thresholds.

Those claims must remain prohibited.
