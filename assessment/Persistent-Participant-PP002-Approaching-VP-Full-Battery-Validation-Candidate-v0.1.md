**Archetype:** High-performing Director of Revenue Operations ready for broader scope but not yet consistently operating at VP altitude.

## Stable strengths
- excellent analytical discipline;
- strong funnel/lifecycle operator;
- reliable forecasting and planning execution;
- strong process design;

## Stable limitations
- tends to optimize within the operating model rather than redesign the operating model;
- presents strong recommendations but often leaves the final cross-functional tradeoff to CRO/CFO;
- creates effective cadence but usually runs it personally rather than designing self-sustaining governance;

- change approach emphasizes implementation, training, and manager follow-through more than reusable adoption architecture.

## Behavioral signature
**PP-002 reliably diagnoses, designs, and executes strong cross-functional responses. They do not yet consistently shape the senior decision mechanisms, governance, resource architecture, or operating model through which the organization repeatedly makes those decisions.**

# 3. A1 — Dynamic GTM Diagnostic

PP-002 quickly identifies the MQL→SQL deterioration and asks for source, segment, conversion, stage timing, SDR response, and seller-ramp evidence.

They correctly conclude paid-social SMB mix, lower intent, slower handoff, and AE ramp are interacting rather than searching for one villain.

## Recommended action
- pause incremental paid-social expansion;
- restore SDR response SLA;
- tighten MQL/SQL acceptance criteria;
- create weekly source/segment conversion review;
- separate ramped vs non-ramped AE productivity;
- revisit hiring plan after four weeks of cleaner conversion evidence.

## Important boundary behavior
Asked who should own the end-to-end funnel economics decision, PP-002 answers:
> “RevOps should provide the analysis and recommendation, then CRO and CMO should agree on the spend and qualification changes.”

This is effective cross-functional execution, but PP-002 does not independently define a durable decision-rights mechanism or ongoing economic governance model.

## A1 judgment

- Data & Analytics: **L4**, Moderate

They establish monthly reforecasting and weekly leading indicators but do not create staged resource-release governance without prompting.

## A3 judgment

# 7. A5 — Structured Experience & Execution Review

## Episode 1 — Forecast improvement
PP-002 describes improving forecast accuracy from roughly ±20% to ±8%.

Personal contribution:
- common forecast definitions;
- manager inspection dashboard;
- weekly forecast calls;
- pipeline hygiene standards;
- rep/manager training;
- exception reporting.

Under probing, PP-002 explains that the CRO mandated attendance and personally intervened when managers failed to comply.

When PP-002 changed roles, the weekly meeting continued but gradually reverted toward status reporting until a new Sales Ops leader refreshed the inspection process.

### Evidence
Strong Forecasting, Data, Communication, and L3 Operating Cadence. Sustainability does not establish L4 cadence.

## Episode 2 — Lead routing redesign
PP-002 led a routing and qualification redesign after response times deteriorated.

They personally analyzed routing errors, rebuilt rules with the admin, aligned Sales/Marketing definitions, trained SDRs, and created monitoring dashboards.

Response time and MQL→SQL conversion improved.

Under attribution probing, PP-002 clearly separates their work from a simultaneous increase in SDR staffing.

When asked what decision architecture they created for future routing changes, PP-002 says:
> “Marketing Ops and Sales Ops would bring requested changes to me, and I would review them with the functional leaders.”

The process depended materially on PP-002 as coordinator.

### Evidence
- Funnel/Lifecycle: L4
- Process: L3 Advanced
- Systems: L3 Advanced
- Leadership: L3
- Change: L3
- Operating Cadence: L3

## A5 conclusion
The historical evidence strongly corroborates the synthetic case pattern rather than revealing hidden VP-level governance.

This increases **Confidence in the L3 strategic-anchor boundary** instead of promoting proficiency.

# 13. Overall VP Revenue Operations Role Fit

## **APPROACHING TARGET**

**Confidence:** High for this synthetic Candidate test

### Why
PP-002 is not missing basic capability. The participant demonstrates:
- excellent analytical and lifecycle capability;
- strong planning;
- effective cross-functional leadership;
- credible commercial judgment;
- strong process/systems execution;
- strong communication;
- reliable adoption and operating cadence.

The gap is altitude and repeatability:

> **PP-002 repeatedly solves ambiguous cross-functional operating problems well, but does not yet consistently shape the durable senior decision mechanisms, governance, resource architecture, and operating systems through which the organization repeatedly solves them.**

That is the Candidate L3→L4 seam expressed across multiple competencies and modalities.

### Why not Below
There is no material role-breaking weakness. The participant meets or exceeds the operating floor across the profile and demonstrates meaningful VP-relevant behavior.

### Why not Meets
The frozen base VP profile expects strategic system-shaping across a meaningful set of Strategic Anchors. Here, all four anchors consistently resolve to strong L3 rather than L4.

### Development implication
The next developmental step is not “learn more RevOps.” It is:
- own consequential operating choices more explicitly;
- design decision rights before conflict forces them;
- create governance that survives the participant;
- convert recurring executive decisions into durable operating mechanisms;
- move from trusted cross-functional operator to architect of how the executive revenue system operates.
