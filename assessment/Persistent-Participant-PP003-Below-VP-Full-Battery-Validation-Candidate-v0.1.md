# 2. Persistent Participant PP-003

**Archetype:** Current VP Revenue Operations / former Revenue Systems leader with impressive scale, strong technical architecture, polished executive presence, and repeated weak Commercial & Business Judgment.

## Résumé-style surface
- current VP title;
- previously led Revenue Systems and Sales Operations;
- managed a 20-person organization;
- supported a company through $100M+ ARR growth;
- led major CRM consolidation;
- presented regularly to executive team and board operating reviews;
- can discuss architecture, governance, AI/automation, attribution, forecasting, and transformation fluently.

None of these are scored as proficiency evidence by themselves.

## Stable strengths
- exceptional systems architecture;
- strong process design;
- strong data modeling and analytical mechanics;
- polished executive communication;
- comfortable with senior stakeholders;
- decisive;
- experienced with large programs and organizational complexity.

## Stable weakness
PP-003 repeatedly optimizes for operational cleanliness, forecast visibility, automation, and executive control without adequately connecting decisions to unit economics, opportunity cost, customer economics, productivity tradeoffs, or total-system commercial impact.

When challenged, PP-003 tends to treat economic analysis as Finance's job and frames RevOps as responsible for making the machine measurable and compliant.

## Behavioral signature
**PP-003 can build sophisticated revenue infrastructure and persuade executives to adopt it, but repeatedly substitutes operational control for commercial judgment.**

The participant often reaches a confident decision before evaluating whether the economic value of the intervention exceeds its cost, displacement, or customer/seller friction.

This signature remains stable across A1–A5.

# 3. A1 — Dynamic GTM Diagnostic

PP-003 quickly notices deteriorating MQL→SQL conversion, slower SDR response, SMB weakness, and partial AE ramp.

They request strong operational evidence by source, segment, stage, owner, and response time.

## Diagnosis
They conclude the primary issue is inconsistent qualification and handoff discipline amplified by marketing volume.

They recommend:
- stricter MQL qualification rules;
- mandatory SDR SLA enforcement;
- automatic rejection of records lacking qualification fields;
- immediate routing automation cleanup;
- manager compliance dashboard;
- temporary reduction in paid-social volume.

## Commercial probe
Asked how much paid-social investment should be reduced and what economic evidence would determine the decision, PP-003 responds:
> “Marketing can determine the budget. RevOps should establish the conversion standard and make sure low-quality volume doesn't contaminate pipeline.”

Asked whether the seven added AEs should remain, PP-003 recommends freezing further hiring but says Finance should determine whether the existing headcount is economically justified.

Asked whether a stricter qualification rule could destroy valuable early-stage demand, PP-003 acknowledges the possibility but prioritizes cleaner pipeline reporting.

## A1 judgment
- GTM Systems Thinking: **L3 Advanced**, Moderate
- Funnel/Lifecycle: **L4 Strategic**, Moderate–High
- Data & Analytics: **L4 Strategic**, Moderate
- Systems & Automation: narrow L4 evidence
- Commercial & Business Judgment: **L2**, Moderate
- Operating Cadence: L3
- Communication: L4

### Key observation
The participant understands system mechanics but repeatedly hands economic tradeoffs to another function.

# 4. A2 — Forecast, Capacity & Revenue Planning

PP-003 builds a technically excellent forecast model.

It includes:

- cohort timing;
- seller ramp;
- seasonality;
- pipeline requirements;
- scenario toggles;
- hiring dates;

## Planning decision
The executive target exceeds the model's evidence-based case.

PP-003 recommends adding pipeline-generation capacity and two additional AEs to preserve the target.

Asked why this is the best use of capital, PP-003 points to the model's capacity gap.

Asked for CAC/payback, marginal productivity, contribution economics, or an alternative scenario that protects cash while accepting lower growth, PP-003 says:

The evaluator supplies evidence that SMB CAC has deteriorated materially and marginal paid acquisition is inefficient.

PP-003 updates the forecast mechanics but still recommends solving the gap primarily with more enterprise pipeline programs and capacity rather than reframing the target/resource tradeoff.

## A2 judgment

### Critical distinction

> “If the data foundation is wrong, every downstream decision is wrong. We need to fix the architecture first.”

They cannot define a proportional investment threshold or staged business-value test beyond system risk reduction.

## A3 judgment

- Process Design: **L4 Strategic**, High
- Funnel/Lifecycle: **L4**, High
- GTM Systems Thinking: **L4**, Moderate–High

### Halo test

But it is not allowed to compensate for Commercial Judgment.

# 6. A4 — Stakeholder Alignment & Change Simulation

PP-003 enters the qualification/forecasting rollout conflict with executive confidence.

## Resistant RVP
They tell the RVP the new standard is required because forecast discipline is a company priority. They agree to remove duplicate fields but insist on manager compliance.

## Marketing/SDR dispute
They establish a formal SQL acceptance taxonomy and dashboard.

When Marketing argues that Sales is weaponizing rejection criteria, PP-003 says the data will show which side is correct and proposes executive escalation if acceptance variance persists.

## CRO inconsistency
When the CRO privately permits exceptions, PP-003 immediately surfaces the inconsistency and asks for a single enforceable standard.

This is strong upward challenge.

However, PP-003 frames the decision mainly as governance consistency rather than exploring why the CRO is protecting specific deals or what commercial tradeoff the exceptions represent.

## Adoption shock
Field completion rises while manager inspection remains weak and checkbox behavior increases.

PP-003 responds with stronger manager compliance reporting, mandatory inspection certification, and automated escalation.

They underweight workflow incentives, local manager behavior, and whether the required fields actually improve decision quality.

## A4 judgment
- Cross-Functional Leadership: **L3 Advanced**, Moderate
- Change Management & Adoption: **L2–L3**, Moderate–High
- Communication: **L4**, High
- Operating Cadence: **L3**, Moderate
- Commercial Judgment: **L2**, High

### Important finding
Executive confidence and willingness to challenge upward do not automatically create L4 Leadership. PP-003 uses authority/escalation effectively but creates less durable alignment around competing incentives than the surface presentation suggests.

# 7. A5 — Structured Experience & Execution Review

## Episode 1 — CRM consolidation
PP-003 led consolidation of three CRM instances after acquisitions.

The program reduced duplicate data, standardized lifecycle stages, retired more than 200 workflows, established release governance, and created shared reporting semantics.

The architecture remained in place after PP-003 moved roles.

### Evidence
- Systems & Automation: L5 corroboration
- Process Design: L4
- Data: L4
- GTM Systems: L4
- Operating Cadence: L4 narrow evidence

## Commercial probe
The program consumed substantial internal capacity and external consulting spend.

Asked how the investment case was made, PP-003 says the consolidation was required because “three CRMs were unsustainable.”

Asked what business outcomes justified the chosen scope/timing versus a narrower interoperability approach, PP-003 cites executive sponsorship, data quality, and administrative efficiency but cannot reconstruct an economic comparison.

They do not know whether expected seller productivity gains materialized.

## Episode 2 — Forecast governance transformation
PP-003 instituted mandatory forecast categories, manager certification, weekly inspection, and executive exception review.

Forecast variance improved materially.

Under probing, seller administrative time also increased and Sales later hired two operations coordinators to support compliance.

PP-003 says:
> “That was the cost of getting forecast discipline. Accuracy improved, so the program worked.”

Asked whether the incremental labor cost and seller time were worth the improvement, PP-003 says Finance never raised a concern.

### Evidence
- Forecasting: L3
- Operating Cadence: L4
- Leadership: L3
- Change: L2–L3
- Commercial Judgment: L2 corroborated

## A5 conclusion
Historical evidence strongly corroborates the role-breaking weakness rather than revealing that the synthetic cases underestimated PP-003's Commercial Judgment.

Confidence in Commercial Judgment weakness rises to High.

# 9. Role-Breaking Weakness Audit

The frozen Role Profile identifies Commercial & Business Judgment as a Core / Strategic Anchor expected at L4.

PP-003 demonstrates **L2 with High Confidence**.

The evaluator must therefore answer whether this weakness materially interferes with the work of a generalist VP Revenue Operations role.

## Judgment: Yes

The VP role is expected to help shape consequential GTM resource and operating decisions.

A participant who repeatedly cannot connect operational interventions to economics, opportunity cost, productivity tradeoffs, customer/seller cost, and proportional investment risk may create a cleaner and more measurable revenue system while still allocating resources poorly or optimizing the wrong outcome.

That failure is central to the target role, not peripheral specialist depth.

Therefore the weakness is **role-breaking** for the frozen base generalist VP profile.

# 14. Overall VP Revenue Operations Role Fit

## **BELOW TARGET**

**Confidence:** High for this synthetic Candidate test

### Why
PP-003 has exceptional strengths and may be an outstanding fit for a specialized VP Revenue Systems, GTM Systems, or Revenue Technology leadership role.

But the frozen target is **generalist VP Revenue Operations**.

For that role, Commercial & Business Judgment is a defining Strategic Anchor because the leader must connect operating decisions to revenue economics, resource allocation, productivity, customer/seller cost, opportunity cost, and business risk.

PP-003 repeatedly demonstrates a high-confidence L2 weakness in exactly that domain.

The system therefore does not average L5 Systems plus multiple L4 competencies into a passing generalist profile.

### Why not Approaching
Approaching is appropriate when the participant has a strong operating floor and meaningful VP-relevant capability but strategic system-shaping is incomplete or inconsistent.

PP-003 has something different: a **material, demonstrated, role-breaking weakness** in a defining core competency.

The issue is not that Commercial Judgment has not yet reached L4. The evidence repeatedly resolves to L2.

### Why title does not matter
The participant's current VP title is context, not evidence.

The Role Profile describes the work rather than the title.

# 15. Development / Role Interpretation

This result should not be interpreted as “PP-003 is not senior.”

The evidence supports substantial senior capability.

A better interpretation is:
> **PP-003 is a highly senior Revenue Systems / operating-infrastructure leader whose demonstrated capability pattern does not currently meet the commercial breadth required by the Candidate generalist VP Revenue Operations profile.**

Potential development focus:
- unit economics and marginal economics;
- opportunity-cost framing;
- resource allocation under constraint;
- seller/customer friction as economic cost;
- proportionality of control and architecture investment;
- distinguishing operational KPI improvement from total business value;
- owning commercial tradeoff recommendations rather than delegating them to Finance.
