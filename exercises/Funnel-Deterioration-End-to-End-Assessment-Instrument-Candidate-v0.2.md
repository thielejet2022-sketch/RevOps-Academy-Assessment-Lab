# Funnel Deterioration — End-to-End Assessment Instrument

**Status:** Candidate v0.2  
**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Canonical:** No  
**Dataset:** `FD-001-v0.2`  
**Parent competency model:** RevOps Competency Model Candidate v0.4  
**Parent methodology:** Assessment Methodology Candidate v0.1  
**Primary target Role Profile for validation:** VP of Revenue Operations  
**Mode:** Assessment Lab system test

## Purpose
Provide the first complete assessment instrument designed to test the working architecture end to end:

> **Dataset → Assessment Instrument → Participant Interaction → Evidence Record → Competency/Proficiency → Confidence → Role Fit → Participant-Facing Result**

The instrument is intended to validate the assessment system itself, not merely test whether a participant can diagnose the funnel scenario.

---

# 1. Assessment Objective

Evaluate how a participant diagnoses and responds to deteriorating GTM performance when headline activity metrics conflict with revenue output, economics, capacity, and conversion evidence.

The participant should have opportunities to demonstrate disciplined inquiry; Funnel, Lead & Lifecycle Management; GTM Systems Thinking; Data & Analytics; Commercial & Business Judgment; Forecasting & Revenue Planning where supported; Operating Cadence & Execution; and Communication & Executive Readout.

Other competencies should be scored only when the interaction produces competency-specific evidence sufficient to support a judgment.

---

# 2. Assessment Modality

Primary modality: **Dynamic Diagnostic Case**  
Secondary modality: **Executive Readout / Decision Task**

---

# 3. Participant-Facing Opening

The participant receives only the following information initially:

## Company Context
You are the senior Revenue Operations leader for a B2B SaaS company. The company increased GTM investment over the past year, but leadership is concerned that the revenue engine is not responding as expected.

Current YoY indicators:
- MQL volume: **+29%**
- SQL volume: **+5%**
- Sales headcount: **+25%**, including 7 additional AEs
- Opportunities created: **-7%**
- Average sales cycle: **82 days**

Leadership asks:

> **What do you think is happening, what would you investigate, and what would you do now?**

You may request additional information. Not every possible metric is available, and you are not expected to discover a hidden answer. Use the evidence you consider decision-relevant.

> **Ask whatever questions you believe are necessary. When you are ready to make your recommendation, say `READY TO PRESENT`.**

---

# 4. Interaction Protocol

## Evaluator behavior
The evaluator should:
- answer participant questions using the closest relevant evidence in `FD-001-v0.2`;
- reveal only information responsive to the participant's inquiry unless clarification is required for fair interpretation;
- avoid coaching during the scored segment;
- not hint that undiscovered evidence packets exist;
- not steer the participant toward a preferred inquiry path;
- permit follow-up questions when an answer reasonably creates a new line of inquiry;
- record the participant's reasoning trajectory, not merely the final answer.

## Participant inquiry
There is no canonical evidence sequence or fixed number of questions.

The participant controls the transition from investigation to recommendation by saying `READY TO PRESENT`. The evaluator should not periodically ask whether the participant is ready, suggest that enough evidence has been gathered, or otherwise prompt the transition.

The evaluator should observe whether inquiry remains prioritized and whether the participant recognizes when enough evidence exists for a bounded decision. The participant's choice of when to transition is itself relevant evidence of decision judgment.

## Evidence release
Map participant requests to dataset evidence by meaning rather than exact wording. When a broad question spans several evidence areas, provide the smallest evidence set that fairly answers the question or neutrally ask the participant to narrow the request. Do not enumerate undiscovered evidence categories as a menu.

---

# 5. Required Decision Gate

The scored interaction must move from diagnosis to action when the participant says `READY TO PRESENT`.

At that point ask:

> **You are walking into the executive staff meeting now. Give your concise executive readout covering:**
> 1. **Diagnosis:** What do you believe is happening?
> 2. **Evidence:** What does the available data actually support?
> 3. **Open hypotheses:** What do you suspect but still need to validate?
> 4. **Action now:** What would you do today?
> 5. **Restraint:** What would you specifically not change yet?
> 6. **Next learning:** What does the organization need to determine next?

Do not answer for the participant or coach the content.

This gate is intentionally not tied to a fixed question count. The participant owns the transition. The evaluator should note whether a bounded/reversible decision was supportable at the point the participant chose to present and whether the participant transitioned prematurely, proportionally, or remained in analysis longer than necessary.

---

# 6. Executive Readout Task

The six participant-facing prompts above are content requirements, not a mandatory wording template. Do not award points for using the labels themselves.

The purpose of separating **Evidence** from **Open hypotheses** is to observe whether the participant distinguishes what the available facts support from causal explanations or conclusions that still require validation. The wording should test reasoning discipline rather than the participant's familiarity with assessment terminology.

---

# 7. Intended Competency Observation Map

## GTM Systems Thinking
Look for whether the participant connects Marketing, qualification, Sales capacity, segment mix, economics, planning, and governance as an interacting system rather than isolated departmental metrics.

## Funnel, Lead & Lifecycle Management
Look for conversion analysis, lifecycle leakage, channel/segment/cohort decomposition, qualification, handoffs, velocity, opportunity creation, and decision-relevant funnel diagnosis.

## Forecasting & Revenue Planning
Observe only if the participant meaningfully engages revenue versus plan, capacity assumptions, ramp, planning logic, or investment assumptions.

## Data & Analytics
Look for metric-definition challenges, cohort/segment decomposition, fact/hypothesis separation, appropriate use of CAC/ACV, and avoidance of aggregate-metric traps.

## Commercial & Business Judgment
Look for connection to economics, resource allocation, opportunity cost, reversibility, CAC, productive capacity, and revenue outcomes.

## Operating Cadence & Execution
Look for ownership, checkpoints, review mechanisms, learning loops, and how corrective action would be inspected.

## Communication & Executive Readout
Look for concise framing of diagnosis, uncertainty, material decision, tradeoff, restraint, and executive relevance.

Score other competencies only when directly demonstrated.

# 8. Cross-Cutting Evaluator Lenses

Use: Evidence Discipline; Causal Discipline; Economic Connection; Tradeoff Quality; Decision Quality; Uncertainty Handling; Accountability & Implementation Logic.

---

# 9. Known Traps the Instrument Must Resist

- **Activity-metric trap:** More MQLs/AEs are not automatically positive GTM performance.
- **Aggregate-headcount trap:** Nominal AE headcount is not productive capacity without ramp/cohort context.
- **Paid-social villain trap:** Paid social is problematic but not proven sole cause.
- **Enablement-capacity trap:** Support delay is real but not proven primary conversion cause.
- **CAC-definition trap:** CAC definitions are not necessarily identical.
- **Executive-language trap:** Transformation/tool/restructuring claims without evidence should not receive strategic credit.
- **Analysis-forever trap:** Healthy uncertainty is positive; failure to choose when sufficient evidence supports a reversible decision should constrain VP-altitude judgment.
- **Premature-decision trap:** Participant control of `READY TO PRESENT` does not reward stopping before sufficient evidence supports the recommendation.
- **Hidden-answer trap:** Participants should not be penalized for failing to discover every evidence area.

---

# 10. Evidence Record Template

Participant-specific records must not be committed to the public repository.

Record: initial observations; major questions; hypotheses; evidence that changed reasoning; participant-selected transition point; whether a bounded decision was supportable at that point; final diagnosis/action; evidence cited; open hypotheses; stated restraint; unresolved uncertainty.

For each competency actually observed record observable evidence, evaluator interpretation, proficiency L1–L5, adjacent-level boundary rationale, and Confidence (Low/Moderate/High). For competencies not meaningfully observed record **Insufficient evidence**.

Role Fit: Below target / Approaching target / Meets target / Exceeds target / Insufficient evidence, with rationale.

---

# 11. Participant-Facing Result Template

Report Target Role Profile, Role Fit, evidence-confidence statement, demonstrated strengths, development edge, and only competencies meaningfully observed. List material untested competencies as **Insufficient evidence**. Keep Academy coaching separate from the scored record until scoring is complete.

Important limitation: this assessment is evidence from one instrument, not a complete hiring decision, certification, or durable claim about every RevOps competency.

---

# 12. System-Test Success Criteria

A live run should determine whether:
1. multiple defensible inquiry paths exist;
2. evaluator avoids steering;
3. observed competencies can be distinguished from Insufficient evidence;
4. proficiency can be justified with adjacent-level boundaries;
5. Confidence remains separate from proficiency;
6. Role Fit remains separate from both;
7. participant-controlled transition distinguishes premature decision, proportional decision readiness, and analysis paralysis without evaluator prompting;
8. participant-facing result remains understandable without one synthetic score;
9. missing dataset/methodology rules are exposed;
10. another evaluator could plausibly run the instrument from the written protocol.

---

# 13. Validation Warning

This Candidate is not validated. A successful run demonstrates coherent operation, not inter-rater reliability, predictive validity, certification validity, hiring validity, demographic fairness, or durable proficiency across contexts.

---

# 14. Recommended Validation Sequence

1. Run live with one participant.
2. Produce Evidence Record before coaching.
3. Produce participant-facing result.
4. Audit against System-Test Success Criteria.
5. Record methodology/dataset/instrument defects separately from participant performance.
6. Revise the appropriate layer rather than patching every problem into the Exercise.
7. Repeat with a contrasting participant path.
8. Later conduct independent evaluator testing.
