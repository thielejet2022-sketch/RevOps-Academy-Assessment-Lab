# Recommended Participant Message

## Invitation

Hi [First Name],

I'm developing and testing a new approach to understanding Revenue Operations capability, and I'd value your help as an early pilot participant.

Rather than a traditional multiple-choice test, you'll work through an interactive RevOps business situation in ChatGPT. You'll investigate what is happening, ask for information you think matters, work through tradeoffs, and make recommendations much as you would in a real operating environment.

This is as much a test of the **assessment itself** as it is an opportunity to observe how you approach RevOps problems. There are no trick questions, and I'm not looking for particular terminology or a memorized framework. I want you to interact naturally and use your own judgment.

### What to expect

- You'll need to be signed into your own ChatGPT account.
- I'll send you a prepared ChatGPT starting link and instructions.
- You'll continue that conversation independently in your own account.
- The pilot will contain a bounded set of RevOps scenarios rather than the full assessment framework.
- You may ask questions and request information as you work through the situation.
- When the assessment is complete, ChatGPT will tell you that the evidence portion has closed and give you instructions for returning the completed conversation to me.
- After review, I'll provide you with a developmental debrief and ask for your feedback on the experience.

Because this is an early pilot, the exact time required is something we're testing too. Please plan for roughly **45–75 minutes**, but don't feel pressure to stretch or shorten your reasoning to fit a clock.

# Before You Participate

Please read the following before beginning.

## Experimental and developmental

This is an experimental RevOps assessment pilot. The methodology is still being developed and tested.

It is **not**:
- a professional certification;
- an employment test;
- a hiring decision;
- a guarantee or prediction of job performance;
- a standardized or independently validated assessment.

No employer will receive a hiring recommendation from your participation in this pilot.

## AI-supported interaction

The assessment is facilitated through ChatGPT. You will interact with an AI facilitator that presents scenarios, responds to your questions, and provides information according to the assessment design.

Your goal is not to guess what the AI wants. Treat the situation as a RevOps problem: investigate, challenge assumptions, ask for evidence, make tradeoffs, and decide what you would do.

## What will be reviewed

Your completed assessment conversation will be used as the evidence record for developmental evaluation and for testing the assessment methodology.

The review may consider:
- the questions you ask;
- evidence you seek;
- how you interpret information;
- assumptions you make or challenge;
- tradeoffs you identify;
- decisions and recommendations you make;
- how your reasoning changes when new evidence appears.

The assessment is intended to evaluate demonstrated reasoning and operating behavior, not whether you use specific RevOps vocabulary.

## Confidential information

Please **do not enter confidential or sensitive information** from your employer, clients, customers, coworkers, or other third parties.

If you refer to your own work experience, generalize or redact company/customer details when needed. Do not provide passwords, credentials, private datasets, trade secrets, personally identifying customer information, or restricted company materials.

The assessment scenarios themselves are designed so that you should not need proprietary information to participate.

## Participant-specific results

Your identifiable assessment results are private by default. They will not be placed in the public RevOps Academy GitHub repository.

De-identified observations from the pilot may later be used to improve the assessment methodology, exercises, scoring guidance, or participant experience. Any public methodology material should be structured so that it does not reasonably identify you.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop the pilot at any point.

If something feels unclear, inappropriate, overly personal, or technically broken, stop and let me know rather than trying to push through it.

Because this is an early pilot, discovering a flaw in the assessment is useful information rather than a failure on your part.

## Developmental debrief

After the assessment evidence is closed and reviewed, you will receive a developmental debrief describing what the pilot observed, including demonstrated strengths, development opportunities, evidence limitations, and areas where the assessment did not collect enough information to support a judgment.

You'll also have an opportunity to tell us what felt realistic, confusing, leading, artificial, or missing. Your feedback is part of the pilot evidence about the assessment itself.
