# Recommended Participant Message

Hi [First Name],

I'm developing and testing a new approach to understanding Revenue Operations capability, and I'd value your help as an early pilot participant.

Rather than a multiple-choice test, you'll work through one interactive RevOps business situation in ChatGPT. You'll investigate what is happening, ask for information you think matters, work through tradeoffs, and make recommendations much as you would in a real operating environment.

This is as much a test of the **assessment itself** as it is an opportunity to observe how you approach a RevOps problem. There are no trick questions, and I'm not looking for particular terminology or a memorized framework. Interact naturally and use your own judgment.

## What to expect

- You'll need to be signed into your own ChatGPT account.
- I'll invite you to a dedicated Shared ChatGPT Project used only for this pilot.
- After joining, open **Projects** in the ChatGPT sidebar, select the assessment Project, then open **START HERE — RevOps Assessment**.
- When prompted, type **READY**.
- The pilot contains one bounded RevOps scenario, A1 Dynamic GTM Diagnostic.
- You may ask questions and request information as you work through the situation.
- Generated graphics/images are outside the supported assessment interaction for this prototype. Please keep requests in normal chat/text/table form.
- When the assessment evidence closes, leave your earlier answers unchanged. The conversation itself is the evidence record.
- After review, I'll provide a developmental debrief and ask for your feedback on the experience.

Please plan for roughly **30–45 minutes**. The time burden itself is something we're testing, so don't stretch or shorten your reasoning to fit the estimate.

## Experimental and developmental

This is an experimental RevOps assessment pilot. The methodology is still being developed and tested.

It is **not** a professional certification, employment test, hiring decision, guarantee of job performance, or standardized independently validated assessment. No employer will receive a hiring recommendation from this pilot.

## AI-supported interaction

The assessment is facilitated through ChatGPT. The AI presents the scenario and responds to your questions according to the assessment design.

Your goal is not to guess what the AI wants. Treat the situation as a RevOps problem: investigate, challenge assumptions, ask for evidence, make tradeoffs, and decide what you would do.

## What will be reviewed

Your completed assessment conversation will be used as the evidence record for developmental evaluation and for testing the assessment methodology. Review may consider the questions you ask, evidence you seek, assumptions you challenge, interpretations, tradeoffs, decisions, recommendations, and how your reasoning changes when new evidence appears.

The assessment is intended to observe demonstrated reasoning and operating behavior, not specific RevOps vocabulary.

## Confidential information

Do **not** enter confidential or sensitive employer, client, customer, coworker, or third-party information. Do not provide passwords, credentials, private datasets, trade secrets, personally identifying customer information, or restricted company materials.

The pilot scenario does not require proprietary information.

## Participant-specific results

Your identifiable assessment results are private by default and will not be placed in the public RevOps Academy GitHub repository.

De-identified observations may later be used to improve the methodology, exercises, scoring guidance, or participant experience, provided they do not reasonably identify you.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any point. If something feels unclear, inappropriate, overly personal, or technically broken, stop and let me know rather than pushing through it.

Because this is an early pilot, finding a flaw in the assessment is useful information.

## Developmental debrief

After the evidence is closed and reviewed, you will receive a developmental debrief describing observed strengths, development opportunities, evidence limitations, and areas where the pilot did not collect enough information to support a judgment.

You'll also be asked what felt realistic, confusing, leading, artificial, or missing. Your feedback is evidence about the assessment itself.
