# RevOps Academy & Assessment Lab — Role Profile Assessment Coverage Matrix

**Status:** Candidate v0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Canonical:** No  
**Parent framework:** RevOps Competency Model Candidate v0.4  
**Parent methodology:** Assessment Methodology Candidate v0.2  
**Architecture assumption:** A credible RevOps profile should be built from a small series of complementary assessments rather than one giant assessment or one test per competency.

## Purpose
Design the smallest practical assessment set capable of producing credible evidence across the eleven RevOps competencies while preserving evidence quality, participant usability, construct separation, and Profile Coverage discipline.

This document designs **coverage**, not individual assessment content.

---

# Core Design Principle

A complete RevOps profile should not depend on one exercise doing everything.

Different capabilities are best observed through different evidence experiences:

> **Diagnose something → Plan something → Design something → Navigate people through something → Show what you have actually done**

The assessment set should create intentional overlap where triangulation is useful without repeating the same competency merely to create more evidence.

A competency may appear in multiple assessments. Repeated high-quality evidence may increase **Confidence**. It does not automatically increase **Proficiency**.

# Candidate Assessment Set

## A1 — Dynamic GTM Diagnostic
**Participant experience:** Diagnose an ambiguous revenue-performance problem as evidence becomes available, then make a bounded executive decision.

**Primary modality:** Dynamic Diagnostic Case  
**Secondary modality:** Executive Readout / Decision Task

Best suited to observe:
- GTM Systems Thinking
- Funnel, Lead & Lifecycle Management
- Data & Analytics
- Commercial & Business Judgment
- Communication & Executive Readout

May also produce evidence for:
- Forecasting & Revenue Planning
- Operating Cadence & Execution

Existing Candidate instrument: Funnel Deterioration End-to-End Assessment Instrument v0.1.

---

## A2 — Forecast, Capacity & Revenue Planning Case
**Participant experience:** Build, challenge, or repair a revenue plan using pipeline, productivity, capacity, ramp, conversion, attainment, and financial assumptions. Respond when assumptions change.

**Primary modality:** Analytical Case  
**Secondary modality:** Dynamic Planning / Decision Task

Best suited to observe:
- Forecasting & Revenue Planning
- Data & Analytics
- Commercial & Business Judgment
- GTM Systems Thinking

May also produce evidence for:
- Operating Cadence & Execution
- Communication & Executive Readout
- Funnel, Lead & Lifecycle Management

Key distinction from A1: A1 diagnoses a deteriorating operating system; A2 requires the participant to construct or challenge forward-looking revenue and capacity logic.

---

## A3 — Revenue Systems & Process Design Case
**Participant experience:** Design or repair a CRM/GTM workflow where lifecycle definitions, routing, data ownership, automation, integrations, exceptions, governance, and business requirements interact.

**Primary modality:** Technical / System-Design Case  
**Secondary modality:** Artifact / Work-Product Review

Best suited to observe:
- Systems & Automation
- Process Design & Optimization
- Funnel, Lead & Lifecycle Management
- GTM Systems Thinking

May also produce evidence for:
- Data & Analytics
- Change Management & Adoption
- Commercial & Business Judgment
- Communication & Executive Readout

Key guardrail: tool configuration trivia is not the assessment. Higher proficiency must be visible through architecture, business fit, governance, reliability, tradeoffs, and complexity management.

---

## A4 — Stakeholder Alignment & Change Simulation
**Participant experience:** Navigate a realistic cross-functional disagreement around a material RevOps change. Stakeholders have competing incentives, incomplete information, resistance, and legitimate constraints.

**Primary modality:** Stakeholder Simulation  
**Secondary modality:** Decision / Communication Task

Best suited to observe:
- Cross-Functional Leadership
- Change Management & Adoption
- Communication & Executive Readout
- Commercial & Business Judgment

May also produce evidence for:
- GTM Systems Thinking
- Operating Cadence & Execution
- Process Design & Optimization

Key guardrail: merely naming stakeholders, creating a RACI, or saying “get alignment” is not sufficient evidence of leadership or change capability. The participant must respond to actual tension, incentives, objections, tradeoffs, and adoption risk.

---

## A5 — Structured Experience & Execution Review
**Participant experience:** Examine one or more real prior operating situations using structured questioning and, where available, artifacts or work products. The participant must distinguish personal contribution from team outcome, reconstruct decisions and constraints, explain what changed, and identify what they would do differently now.

**Primary modality:** Structured Behavioral / Work-History Evidence  
**Secondary modalities:** Artifact / Work-Product Review; Longitudinal evidence when available

Best suited to strengthen evidence for:
- Operating Cadence & Execution
- Cross-Functional Leadership
- Change Management & Adoption
- Communication & Executive Readout

May strengthen evidence for any competency actually demonstrated in the selected experience, including:
- Forecasting & Revenue Planning
- Systems & Automation
- Process Design & Optimization
- Commercial & Business Judgment
- GTM Systems Thinking

Key guardrail: résumé claims and reported outcomes are starting points for inquiry, not proficiency evidence by themselves.

# What the Matrix Reveals

## 1. Four cases are not enough for a strong full-profile claim
A1–A4 can create broad scenario-based coverage, but **Operating Cadence & Execution**, **Cross-Functional Leadership**, and **Change Management & Adoption** contain an important real-world dimension that simulations alone may not establish with strong Confidence.

A5 exists primarily to address the difference between:

> **what a participant says they would do**

and

> **what they can demonstrate they have actually done and sustained.**

This does not mean prior experience is required for every Academy learner. It means stronger role-level claims, especially hiring-oriented claims, may require evidence beyond hypothetical cases.

## 2. The five experiences do not need equal weight or equal length
This is not a five-exam certification sequence.

A1–A4 may be relatively bounded experiences. A5 may be a structured interview/review rather than a traditional test.

The set should be modular. Academy Mode may use individual assessments independently. Assessment Lab Mode may combine them when broader Profile Coverage is needed.

## 3. Overlap is intentional, but not unlimited
GTM Systems Thinking and Commercial & Business Judgment appear across several experiences because they are integrative capabilities.

Systems & Automation remains concentrated in A3 because forcing systems evidence into unrelated exercises would create artificial scoring.

Cross-Functional Leadership and Change Management remain concentrated in A4/A5 because talking about stakeholders in an analytical case is weak behavioral evidence.

## 4. Communication is observable often, but should not become a polish tax
Communication appears in several experiences because participants must explain decisions in RevOps work.

Do not let writing style, charisma, executive vocabulary, accent, personality, or presentation polish inflate unrelated competency scores.

Communication & Executive Readout should be scored only on the distinct communication behavior required by that competency.

# Candidate Minimum Assessment Architecture by Use Case

## Academy practice
Use **one assessment at a time** based on the capability being developed.

No full-profile claim is necessary.

## Developmental profile
Use a selected subset, commonly **A1 + A3 + A4**, then add A2 or A5 depending on the learner's development goals.

Report Profile Coverage explicitly. Do not imply a complete role assessment.

## Serious role-fit / hiring-oriented assessment
Candidate default:

> **A1 + A2 + A3 + A4 + A5**

This is the current strongest coverage architecture, but it is not yet validated as the minimum required battery.

For consequential role-level claims, use Assessment Methodology v0.2 rules for Observed Role Fit, Overall Role Fit, Profile Coverage, Confidence, and evidence burden.

# Candidate Evidence Expectations

A competency does not need a Primary mark in two assessments to be scored.

However, broader/high-stakes claims should consider:
- whether the competency was observed in a modality suited to the construct;
- whether evidence was direct or self-reported;
- whether the evidence distinguished adjacent proficiency levels;
- whether a second modality materially improves Confidence;
- whether contradictory evidence exists;
- whether the competency is central to the target Role Profile.

Do not establish a mechanical rule such as “two assessments per competency” without validation evidence.

---

# Profile Coverage Reporting

For any multi-assessment profile, report:

## Materially observed
Competencies with sufficient evidence to support a proficiency judgment.

## Narrowly observed
Competencies for which a provisional judgment may exist but evidence breadth or modality limits Confidence.

## Insufficient evidence
Competencies not meaningfully observed.

Do not report Profile Coverage as a percentage in Candidate v0.1.

Do not treat more coverage as better participant performance. Coverage measures the assessment, not the person.

---

# Efficiency Pressure Test

The five-assessment architecture should be challenged before being treated as necessary.

Questions:
1. Can A2 Forecast/Planning be partially combined with A1 without weakening either construct?
2. Can A3 Systems/Process legitimately expose enough Change Management to reduce burden on A4? Probably not for high-confidence behavioral claims.
3. Can A5 be optional in Academy Mode but required for high-stakes hiring use? Likely yes.
4. Can A4 and A5 together provide stronger Cross-Functional Leadership evidence than either alone? Likely yes.
5. What is the shortest participant experience that still creates useful evidence rather than assessment fatigue?
6. Which Role Profiles require less breadth than the VP profile?

Do not optimize for minimum time at the expense of trustworthy evidence.

---

# Validation Plan

Before accepting this coverage architecture as stable:

1. pressure-test the matrix for missing or artificially duplicated competencies;
2. design one Candidate instrument for A2, A3, and A4;
3. design a structured evidence protocol for A5 rather than an unstructured résumé interview;
4. run synthetic coverage tests using contrasting participant profiles;
5. inspect whether competencies are being double-counted across instruments;
6. test whether Profile Coverage and Confidence behave sensibly as assessments accumulate;
7. eventually run real participants and independent evaluators.
